# Rooms and spaces in an rvt-rs IFC export

This page is for the importer of an rvt-rs export: what an `IfcSpace` holds for each release, the one property that says whether to trust its area, what is measured against Revit's own export and where, and what is not. Every `IfcSpace` rvt-rs writes in a geometry export (the default) carries that property: rooms, Revit Spaces, family instances written as spaces and Areas alike. An export with no geometry (`typed_no_geometry`) has no outlines and no property. The evidence is in [RE-313](../reports/element-framing/RE-313-space-outlines-measured.md) and, for the rooms whose stored solid the first reader did not close, [RE-316](../reports/element-framing/RE-316-room-solids-resolved.md); the earlier findings are RE-29, RE-101, RE-102, RE-117, RE-153, RE-196, RE-255 and RE-296, and RE-191 and [RE-317](../reports/element-framing/RE-317-area-boundaries.md) for Areas.

## Which `IfcSpace`s rvt-rs writes

| Source | `IfcSpace` | Body |
|---|---|---|
| A Revit room | `Name` is the room's number, `LongName` its name, `PredefinedType` `SPACE`, in the storey of the Level its record names | the outline of the room's stored solid, or the record's bounding box (below) |
| A Revit area (Revit 2024 to 2027; Revit 2015 to 2023 only where the body is proven, RE-324, RE-333) | the same naming | the region of Revit's own plan topology for the Area where that is read and passes its checks (RE-333), else the region its area boundary lines enclose around its point, up to the Level above it (below); none where neither is proven: the space then has no `Representation` |
| A Revit Space (MEP), in a model that has one | `IfcSpace` without a predefined type; no measurement singles one out | its record's box (`record_box`) unless a carrier draws more (`family_body`) |
| A family instance whose "Export to IFC As" names `IfcSpace` (#763) | named `Family:Type:ElementId` as Revit's export names other family instances | its record's box, turned with the instance (`record_box`), until a carrier draws its family. Where its family draws no model form, Revit's export leaves the instance out, and rvt-rs will too once PR #1279 (RE-312, for #1262, parking bays) lands |

A room carries its ElementId (`RvtElementRecordGeometry.ElementId`), its number and name (`RoomNumber`, `RoomName`), `Pset_SpaceCommon.Reference` (name and number) and `IsExternal` where measured, and `Pset_SpaceCoveringRequirements.FloorCovering` where the room's Floor Finish is read. A room's `Qto_SpaceBaseQuantities.Height` is written where its record holds the solid's faces (RE-255) and its `NetVolume` where every vertical face of the solid runs from floor to ceiling, on Revit 2024 and 2025 (RE-296). `Qto_SpaceBaseQuantities` never holds an area: take the area from the body.

## Which property says whether to trust the area

Read `RvtElementRecordGeometry.SpaceOutlineSource` on the `IfcSpace`:

| Value | The body is | Its plan area is |
|---|---|---|
| `stored_solid` | the outline of the room's stored solid, extruded to the room's height, read by the exact walk of RE-101, with each round column the room surrounds taken off as a void and one against a wall as a notch (RE-334) | Revit's room area: within 0.1% of Revit's export on every `stored_solid` room scored below by the scorer's upward faces except one, the permit office's STAIRCASE room 2611271 at 66%, which by Revit's floor is within 0.1% like the others (221 of 221 as built, 222 of 222 at permit; RE-313 §2, §3, RE-316 §3) |
| `stored_solid_faces` | the outline of the room's stored solid, read from the faces its solid lists, up to the solid's own end, where the walk did not close them: their edges close as the walk reads them (RE-316) | Revit's room area: within 0.1% on 9 of 9 office rooms as built, by every metric. Bounded by the solid's list of objects and a gap, not by the walk, so a closed loop of another element's faces inside the room's box would be read as the room's: that is why it is labelled apart (RE-316 §2, §3) |
| `stored_solid_approximate` | the same, as the boundary of a region, a column that overlaps a wall taken off it or an end of one wall edge lying on another, or up to 0.02 ft short of the room's record box on a side | approximate. Of the office's 15 room scores (4 as built, 11 at permit) 10 are within 0.1% of Revit's floor area and 5 are 0.45% to 0.68% under it (four rooms, 1671995 in both files): Revit's export does not take such a column off the room, and nothing in the file says which. The room schedule leaves the area blank (RE-316 §2, §3) |
| `record_box` | the record's bounding box (turned with a family instance that is turned) | the box's area, not the space's. It equals the space's only when the space is a rectangle; the area is Revit's on none of the office's room scores that keep their box (1 room at permit, 3095831; RE-316 §4) |
| `saved_mesh` | the mesh Revit saved for the room (`rvt-ifc --saved-meshes`) | the mesh's; not scored |
| `area_plan_topology` | the loop of Revit's own plan topology for the Area: its area boundary lines moved by their area rule's offsets (to a wall's face, say), arcs written as arcs (three-point `IfcIndexedPolyCurve` arcs, a span over a half turn as two), met where the moved lines cross, its holes the components it encloses, extruded from its Level to the Level above (RE-333). Used first, wherever it is read and passes its checks: the rebuilt loop has the area Revit saves for its circuit (`PlanCircuit m_area`, to 1e-6) and holds the Area's point in no hole. A point where a third line meets a straight outline is a vertex, as in `area_boundary` | Revit's area space: on `rac_advanced`'s 2020 save all 25 Areas are within 0.1% in area (worst 2.0e-7) and within 0.01 ft in outline of the three.js IFC2X3 export, which nothing of the topology was read off; the office's 30 have the regions and the vertex sets their `area_boundary` had, ring for ring: as built the outline starts at another vertex of the same ring in 3 of them and the holes of 6 come in another order (at permit, 2 and 6) (RE-333 section 8). The export is IFC2X3, counted apart, never used to remove a value. Measured on the archived head `e9124b3a` of #1371 (Measure runs 41894715743 and 41894722135), not yet on the head that lands: the unfiltered Measure run named on pull request #1428 replaces these figures. |
| `area_boundary` | the region that the lines of the Area's scheme on its Level enclose around its point, its holes the loops directly inside, extruded from its Level up to the Level above it, both building stories | Revit's area space: within 0.1% of Revit's export, within 0.01 ft in outline, and with its lowest and highest vertex within 0.01 ft, on 60 of the 60 Area scores below, the office counted at both stages (RE-317 §5) |
| `area_boundary_joined` | the same region, where its outline runs along a stretch of a boundary line carried on to the line it joins, or along a connector between two parallel lines that stop at the same place (RE-330); written only where the plan topology is not read or fails a check, and on `rac_advanced` it is read, so none of its Areas says it since RE-333 (they say `area_plan_topology`) | as `area_boundary`, within 0.1% in area and 0.01 ft in outline of Revit's: the only external check of an Area drawn from lines carried on to their joins is an IFC2X3 export of Autodesk's `rac_advanced` from the three.js repository, by a build of Revit 2021's exporter whose string ends "IU alternativa 21.0.0.383" (publisher not established), showing the project's 2015 to 2020 state, against which the joins were read off and then scored (in-sample); the 2022 and 2023 saves are also checked against the 2024 save's reader, and no Revit IFC4 export of these Areas exists (RE-330). Never used to remove a value or to settle a disagreement between exporters |
| `none` | no body (an Area whose boundary is not proven) | not available |
| `family_body` | a Revit Space or a family instance written as an `IfcSpace`, drawn as more than its box; its `BodySource` says how | not scored |

A room's `BodySource` is `partition_element_record_bbox` whichever of the stored-solid or `record_box` values it has, because the placement and the height always come from the record's box: read `SpaceOutlineSource`, not `BodySource`. An export from before this property (rvt-rs 0.12.x and earlier) says the same through `ProfileSource` (`partition_room_solid_faces`, and since RE-316 `partition_room_solid_faces_resolved` and `partition_room_solid_faces_approximate`) and `ProfileResolved`. A room with `stored_solid_faces` or `stored_solid_approximate` carries no `NetVolume`.

The export also counts the spaces drawn as their record's box (rooms and family instances alike), so an importer can check a whole file before reading a space:

- `exported.bounding_box_spaces` in the diagnostics JSON (`rvt-ifc --diagnostics`), out of `exported.by_ifc_type.IFCSPACE`;
- `exported.resolved_outline_spaces` (`stored_solid_faces`) and `exported.approximate_outline_spaces` (`stored_solid_approximate`), the rooms whose outline was not read by the exact walk;
- a line in `rvt-ifc`'s summary for each: "N of M spaces are their record's box", "N rooms take an outline resolved from the faces up to their stored solid's end", "N rooms take an approximate outline".

Areas as spaces, with a body where their boundary lines prove one, are read on Revit 2024 to 2027 and, by the same rule, on Revit 2015 to 2023. An Area's region is the loop Revit's own plan topology keeps for it where that is read and passes its checks (RE-333; it says `area_plan_topology`): its area boundary lines moved by their area rule's offsets, arcs included, checked against the area Revit saves for the loop and against the Area's point. Otherwise it is the region the lines' arrangement gives: a line that stops short of the line it joins is carried on to it, and two parallel lines that stop at the same place a wall's thickness apart are joined by a connector, where their records name each other (RE-330). A group of lines that cannot be read as a whole (a line in a non-primary design option, at a stray height, with copies that disagree, or missing) gives no region of either kind. On Revit 2025 to 2027 the Areas are checked against the same project's 2024 save (Autodesk's `rac_advanced`: the same 25 Areas, numbers, names, storeys and bodies on each). A Snowdon Towers 2025 file awaits a recorded source. On the recorded Autodesk 2024 Snowdon Towers samples rvt-rs writes 101 Area spaces (96 with a body) on Architectural and 7 (2 with a body) on Structural, where main writes none (RE-333 section 8). Not measured against any Revit export (Griffin, 2026-11-05, "Ship, labelled, with a gate"): the Areas of Revit 2025 to 2027 and the 108 Area spaces of the recorded Autodesk 2024 Snowdon Towers samples (101 Architectural, 7 Structural, counted at `e9124b3a`). They are written by default and say `SpaceOutlineSource` `area_plan_topology`. The evidence is that the loop drawn for each equals the area Revit saves in the file for that loop (`PlanCircuit m_area`, to 1e-6) and holds the Area's point and, on 2025 to 2027, the same project's 2024 save gives the same Areas. They stay written only if the held-out run shows no Area written that Revit's export lacks (`only in rvt-rs` 0) on every Revit 2025 to 2027 model with Areas. Measured on the archived head `e9124b3a` of #1371 (Measure runs 41894715743 and 41894722135), not yet on the head that lands: the unfiltered Measure run named on pull request #1428 replaces these figures. On Revit 2015 to 2023 an Area is written only where its region and body are proven; the others are not written and are counted by reason in `skipped` (`area_without_proven_body`, RE-324). On Autodesk's `rac_advanced` all 25 Areas are drawn on every save but the 2021 one, which holds two copies of 16 lines and is refused (RE-324). What those Areas rest on: the only external check of an Area drawn from lines carried on to their joins is an IFC2X3 export of Autodesk's `rac_advanced` from the three.js repository, by a build of Revit 2021's exporter whose string ends "IU alternativa 21.0.0.383" (publisher not established), showing the project's 2015 to 2020 state, against which the joins were read off and then scored (in-sample); the 2022 and 2023 saves are also checked against the 2024 save's reader, and no Revit IFC4 export of these Areas exists (RE-330). The plan topology's Areas were not read off that export: the 25 Areas of the 2020 save are all within 0.1% in area and 0.01 ft in outline of it, with none extra and none missing, and the 16 that the joins draw equal their topology loops on every save (RE-333 section 8). The first rule for 2015 to 2023, writing every placed Area as 2024 does, wrote spaces Revit's export lacks on a held-out project (below).

`ROOM_SOLID_SUPPORTED_REVIT_VERSIONS` in `src/research/partition_room_boundaries.rs` is the list of releases whose room solids are read.

## What is measured, and where

`tools/re/space_profiles_vs_ifc.py` meshes both exports, moves them into Revit's internal feet and compares the plan union of each space's upward faces. It prints four figures, each on its own line:

- **area**: the plan area, within 0.1% of Revit's;
- **boundary**: the largest distance from a point of either outline to the other's outline, within 0.01 ft. This is the room's outline, and the figure used for "outline" in the table and in the text below;
- **outline**: the largest distance from a vertex of either mesh's upward faces to the other's outline, within 0.01 ft. Revit meshes a ceiling step inside a room as an upward face of its own, and its vertices count against this one, so it reads some correct outlines as wrong (RE-313 §3). The CI gate pins both. These three read the faces of Revit's mesh that point up, within 25 degrees of vertical, so a room under a stepped or sloped ceiling is read as the flat part of it: `tools/re/space_overlaps_vs_ifc.py` also measures the plan area of each space's floor (its downward faces) and of every face projected, in both exports, and counts the rooms within 0.1% of Revit's by each (RE-316 §3). The floor is the room's plan.
- **height**: the lowest and the highest vertex of either mesh, within 0.01 ft: the base and the top of the space, so its height. It is printed on its own line and for each kind of space (RE-317); it is 306 of 306 spaces in both exports on the office as built and 308 of 308 at building permit.

Measure runs the scorer on every reference model (`-f scorers=space_profiles_vs_ifc`); the IfcOpenShell job of CI runs it on the first two rows, pairing by GlobalId (`--match globalid`), and fails below their counts in area, outline and boundary.

## What is not measured

- **Areas** (RE-317, RE-324), measured against Revit's export on the BIM4LCA office only:
  - Revit 2015 to 2023 against a Revit export: one project, Autodesk's `rac_advanced`, against an IFC2X3 export from the three.js repository, by a build of Revit 2021's exporter whose string ends "IU alternativa 21.0.0.383" (publisher not established), showing the 2015 to 2020 state of the model (RE-330): the Areas drawn on its 2020 save are within 0.1% in area and 0.01 ft in outline of it, and, with the plan topology (RE-333), all 25 Areas of the 2020 save are written, within 0.1% in area and 0.01 ft in outline, none extra and none missing (RE-333 section 8). The topology's loops and figures are not read off this export, so that agreement is out of sample; the joins of RE-330 were, which was in-sample, and they equal the topology's loops on 16 of 16. The 2022 and 2023 saves are checked against the 2024 save's reader, which differs from the export by the 16 lines the sample was edited on (0.0246 ft). `rst_basic` (2016 to 2023) is read like its 2024 save, and the held-out BIM Whale project is a regression check, quoted by counts only (RE-324). No Revit IFC4 export of a 2015 to 2023 Area exists. An Area there is written only where its body is proven, so all 25 of `rac_advanced`'s Areas are written but on its 2021 save, where none is (two copies of 16 lines disagree, so the group is not read, and Revit's plan topology is not asked: `skipped`, `area_without_proven_body`). Where the plan topology gives the region, the area Revit saves for the loop is Revit's figure in the file, not Revit's export. Revit 2008 to 2014 are not read, and a 2015 Area that names a design option is left out;
  - Revit 2025 to 2027 and the recorded Snowdon Towers 2024 samples, whose Areas are written against no Revit export, labelled (Griffin, 2026-11-05, "Ship, labelled, with a gate"): the loop drawn for each equals the area Revit saves in the file for that loop (`PlanCircuit m_area`, to 1e-6) and holds the Area's point and, on 2025 to 2027, the same project's 2024 save gives the same Areas; the gate is `only in rvt-rs` 0 on every held-out Revit 2025 to 2027 model with Areas;
  - the top storey: an Area on the highest Level has no body, where Revit draws it one unit high;
  - Levels that are not building stories: an Area has a body only where its Level and the Level above are both read as building stories and no other Level lies between them or at either elevation. All ten of the office's Levels are storeys, so every Area there meets that, and a Level that breaks it is unmeasured;
  - the Level's "Story Above", which Revit's exporter takes first for an Area's height and rvt-rs never reads: the office's heights equal the distance to the next Level up, so a Level whose Story Above is another Level is unmeasured;
  - an Area's base offset, read as zero;
  - Areas bounded by walls: none of the 25 Areas that Revit's export of `rac_advanced` draws is (RE-330): the 4 once taken to be are bounded by area boundary lines whose area rule measures them 0.497 ft from the line, to the walls' faces, and Revit's plan topology gives their region (RE-333). An Area whose lines do not close and whose plan topology is not read or fails a check is not written;
  - boundary lines that are not straight along the plan axes are not read, and an Area whose region the box of one touches is refused. Of `rac_advanced`'s four, one is a straight line 0.016 degrees off the axis, two are straight lines on a wall's centreline whose record box is 0.497 ft wider than the line (Revit's outline runs along the line on one side of a vertical line and along the box's far edge, the wall's face, on the other), and one is an arc (RE-330 section 5); the plan topology draws them, and where it is not read they refuse the Area as before (RE-333). The office has none;
  - `Qto_SpaceBaseQuantities.Height`, which Revit writes on an Area and rvt-rs does not yet.
  The diagnostics count the Areas without a body by reason (`exported.area_body_refusals`).
- Revit 2008 to 2020 and 2023 on any model Measure can run, beyond the held-out counts above: no reference model with a Revit room export exists for them in the corpus.
- Revit Spaces (MEP): no measurement singles one out.
- Room outlines of Revit 2026 and 2027 beyond the rectangles: the held-out house's 13 rooms are all within 0.01 ft of Revit's in area, boundary and outline (counts only, one house; the rows above). No other 2026 or 2027 project with a Revit export has rooms that are not rectangles (#1260).
- A room's `Height` and `NetVolume` as scored by `tools/re/qtos_vs_ifc.py`, not by this scorer.
- Revit's phase filter. A room's phase is `RoomElem.m_phaseId` (not its created phase, which is unset; RE-326), and Revit's exporter leaves out a room whose phase is not the export phase, the project's last unless the export setup or a view names another. rvt-rs does the same: such a room is not written, the diagnostics count it in `skipped` as `room_not_in_export_phase` with the phase it sits in, and `rvt-ifc --phase <name|id>` chooses another export phase. A room whose phase is unset, unread or not one of the project's is written, and so is an Area or an MEP space. Measured: all 621 rooms of the 12 models with a Revit export are in the last phase and in Revit's export, so none of them is changed (unfiltered Measure, run 41784044966: of 111 exports 12 move, the `rac_basic_sample_project` saves of Revit 2016 to 2027, and 99 are byte for byte as before, the held-out models included). Unmeasured: a Revit export made with the default setup that leaves a room out for its phase, because no project in the corpus has one. The only room outside its last phase is in Autodesk's `rac_basic_sample_project` (14 rooms in Working Drawings, the second of three phases); a public Revit 2018 export of it holds all 14, which rvt-rs writes with `--phase "Working Drawings"` (14 of that export's 17 `IfcSpace` by GlobalId, no other entity changing) and leaves out by default (Measure runs 41784415396 and 41784419457). Revit 2015 and earlier write every room, as every element: both phase rules start at Revit 2016, because the element rule's only evidence on a 32-bit file is a Revit 2018 project (RE-315) and a room rule that reached further than the element rule would stand on less. Autodesk's `rac_basic_sample_project` saved by Revit 2015 therefore keeps its 14 rooms (#1332).
- Rooms resolved as `stored_solid_faces` or `stored_solid_approximate` (RE-316) on any release but Revit 2024: the evidence is one building in two states and one held-out project. No face of another element inside the room's box is excluded by anything but the solid's list of objects and a gap of 1,024 bytes (RE-316 §2). Round columns (RE-334): the arcs of vertical cylinders that reach the floor are edges of a room's outline, so a column is a void of it; two Revit exports of rooms with one draw every column as a void: the IFC2X3 export of `rac_advanced` (the reader and the export have the same number of voids in 107 of 107 spaces, 48 of them with voids) and the public Revit 2018 IFC4 export of `rac_basic` (room 101, one void; the reader moves it from 1.710% to 0.628% over Revit's area, read outside Measure); the exports of the office, Core Interior, RE1, the R2025 models and the held-out files (53 models) are byte for byte as before. Revit's own area for `rac_basic` room 101 (its `GSA Space Areas`, 781.08 ft²) is the geometry with the column taken off. Unmeasured: Revit's own area for the `rac_advanced` rooms (its export carries none), and the 4.95 ft² still over in `rac_basic` room 101's outer ring. 49 of the 50 `rme` rooms (stepped floors and ceilings) and Snowdon Towers' seven keep their box.

## Check it yourself

```bash
rvt-ifc model.rvt -o model.ifc --diagnostics model.diagnostics.json
python3 tools/re/space_profiles_vs_ifc.py model.ifc revit-export.ifc --list 10
```

The second command needs Revit's own IFC export of the same model, IfcOpenShell, NumPy and Shapely (`tools/ci/requirements-ifc.txt`). It prints the counts above, one line each (`area`, `boundary`, `outline`, and the same split by what the space is and its `SpaceOutlineSource`), and for every space off in area or outline, the area on both sides, the distances and `SpaceOutlineSource`.
