# Native world-model projection boundaries

Each native projection has its own coverage and refusal diagnostics. One
successful projection does not establish completeness of another.

## Connector networks

Connector inventories preserve owner and port identities, directed edge kinds,
target modes, resolution state, reciprocity state, and unresolved references.
Physical cross-owner edges, same-owner edges, logical system references, and
unresolved saved references remain distinct. A saved connector edge is not
automatically a canonical `feeds`, `flows_to`, or `connected_to` relationship.
World-frame alignment remains explicit.

## Saved graphics

Channel-103 saved graphics are separate from operation-derived geometry. The
bounded path can preserve current-owner graphics, shared-symbol graphics,
trimmed faces, faceted meshes, normals, transforms, materials, and diagnostics
for open loops, malformed chains, unsupported surfaces, and missing owners.
Decoded geometry does not establish current liveness, complete graphics
selection, or universal geometry parity.

Supported analytic cylindrical surfaces preserve center, radius, basis,
orientation, trim, and a bounded mesh. Unsupported holes, seams, nonfinite
values, invalid bases, and unsupported tessellation budgets are refused.
Multiple saved loops remain unsupported until their roles are known. Filtered
graphics and malformed graphics remain separate status outcomes.

The native world-model report keeps partial and unsupported projections visible
and does not permit a complete-world-model claim.
