# Evidence

The measured figures behind the [README](../README.md), moved here verbatim so the README can stay a short landing page: what each Revit release gives by input, what works today layer by layer, what rvt-rs reads from real projects against Revit's own exports, and what does not work yet. [`support-matrix.json`](support-matrix.json) is the machine-readable ceiling for every claim, and `rvt-capabilities --matrix -f text` prints it.

Before the README was shortened, its "Support and evidence" section said the following; "this README" there means the figures on this page.

Support depends on the Revit release, the file type and the feature. Opening a file and reading its metadata does not mean its elements or geometry can be recovered: elements are recovered from Revit 2023, 2024 and 2025 projects, and experimentally from Revit 2008 to 2022, 2026 and 2027 projects (2008 to 2014 outside the 1.0 range, on local files only). Geometry is exact where the file's bytes are decoded and the element's bounding box elsewhere. `rvt-elements` and Python list each element's saved parameters on Revit 2023 to 2027 files, and experimentally on 2015 to 2022 ones, where some records are refused (RE-281), and `rvt-ifc --revit-property-sets` writes the named ones in Revit's own property sets (on Autodesk's Snowdon Towers HVAC, every one Revit's; on puzzbobb's Revit 2025 models, scored in CI, 2,502 of 2,646). Every export comes with diagnostics that say what was approximated or left out; check them before relying on an export for measurement or coordination.

The recovery figures in this README compare rvt-rs's export with Revit's own IFC export of the same file, element by element, on the models named in [What rvt-rs reads from real projects](#what-rvt-rs-reads-from-real-projects). Other models of the same releases are expected to behave alike but are not measured, and files saved by the same release can store their data differently. [`docs/support-matrix.json`](support-matrix.json) records each capability with its evidence and its status ceiling. A "Measure run" is a run of the repository's [Measure workflow](../.github/workflows/measure.yml), which exports the redistributable reference models on GitHub's runners and scores them against Revit's own exports with the scorers in `tools/re/`; an RE number is a report in [`reports/element-framing/`](../reports/element-framing/).

**Typical uses:** inventory and audit folders of Revit files without Revit (`rvt-info`); open a 2024 or 2025 model in a browser or in an IFC viewer to check its layout, storeys, types and materials; take element and room schedules into a spreadsheet (`rvt-schedule`); feed IFC or glTF into a coordination, quantity or visualisation pipeline, checking the per-export diagnostics first; and research the format itself with the probes under `examples/`.

`rvt-capabilities --matrix -f text` prints the support matrix; [`docs/status.md`](status.md) and [`docs/supported-profile.md`](supported-profile.md) summarise it.

## What rvt-rs reads from real projects

On Revit 2024 and 2025 project files, rvt-rs reads each element from its partition record: its ElementId, `BuiltInCategory`, bounding box and references, for a family instance its family and type, and for a wall, floor, ceiling, roof or railing its type's name.
