- The last wall of the list that stands at a height: it gives 3490093 on 2057668 (Revit: 1493966), 1493966 on 2057670 (Revit: 3490093) and 1347201 on 2058499 and 2058501 over 179.298 to 179.790 ft (Revit: 3490093). The least ElementId: 1493966 on 2057670 (Revit: 3490093) and 1347201 on 2058499 and 2058501 (Revit: 3490093); it fits 2057668 only.
- RE-337's bound by the wall beneath's lowest underside. 1493966 (180.610 ft) and 1347201 (179.790 ft) have their tops cut in steps by floors, and Revit steps all four walls at their own top, 182.087 ft. The pieces of the stepped top (RE-320) that stand over the end below the wall's own top are what make the wall stand there; a wall of which that is not known is refused.
- Whether Revit's 3490093 yields where 2057668's end enters it (y 75.418 to 75.613, 179.298 to 182.087 ft). It does not: Revit's body of 3490093 has no notch there, the two bodies overlap. The strips of an end may therefore enter a wall the record joins; a wall the record does not join that stands in one refuses the end.

## 3. The rule

For a wall with an edited elevation profile (a `WallProfileOpening` voids it) that is a plain rectangle along a model axis, has `JoinTrimStart` and `JoinTrimEnd`, and whose record lists walls at an end (`m_wall_end_joins_start`, `m_wall_end_joins_end` and the curve `m_wall_curve`, from `native_walls::EndJoins`): the end of the wall's body is the end whose box end (the body's end moved out by the trim) lies at the curve's start or end. `read_end` walks the list in order over the heights from the wall's base to its top. For each wall of the list that stands over a height not yet taken (a wall drawn as its plan's prism, or as RE-320's stepped body, up to its own top), the wall must be perpendicular, must span the end's width at its face and must hold the end's line inside its thickness; the end over those heights is its face, measured over its pieces. The end is then drawn as the wall's own plan to its top and one strip, from the base, for each distance the end reaches beyond the next lower one, each to the height the walls it follows stand to (`strips_of`): the body of a wall with an end that is longer below than above, as all four are, is these columns. A wall whose cut base (RE-336, RE-349) does not resolve over the longer plan, or whose solid cannot be made, keeps its end, as before. A joined wall whose base join names elements (`PendingBase`) is not read as standing: the cut of its base runs after this pass, and a floor that raises it would leave it standing from higher than the record box's base that `followed_wall` takes (none of the four joined walls has a base join; a wall that has one is `joined_wall_not_read` where it matters). The rule applies to Revit 2024 only, on its own list (`WALL_END_JOIN_REVIT_VERSIONS`, `[2024]`, a decision of its own: the profile openings it asks for are read on a list that may grow). Whether the wall's line ends inside a joined wall, and how thick that wall is, are read from the joined wall's whole plan once for all its heights (a wall joins as a whole; the pieces above the lowest underside of its stepped top are parts of that plan), and the face at each height from the slabs that stand there. `m_joinedEnd` of each list entry is not read, so that the ends the list says the walls join by are unverified. A wall of the list that is not read refuses the end before the heights it stands over are known (it may stand anywhere the end is read over), even where it would stand outside them.

The end is kept, with one `BodyEndRefusal`, where: a wall of the list that may stand over a remaining height is not read (`joined_wall_not_read`), where its top is not known there (`joined_wall_top_not_read`: a floor cut it whole and its own top is not recorded, or no piece of its stepped top stands over the end below its own top), where it is not a perpendicular wall the end meets (`joined_wall_does_not_meet_the_end`), where the walls of the list do not stand over the whole of the wall's height (`joined_walls_do_not_reach_the_wall_height`), where a joined wall's face is inside the end the box gives (`joined_wall_face_inside_the_wall`: strips only add), where the end would reach further at a greater height than below (`joined_wall_faces_overhang_the_lower_end`), or where a wall the record does not join stands in a strip (`wall_stands_in_the_added_end`), where the record trimmed an end and no curve end lies at the box's end or no list is read for it (`end_join_curve_not_at_the_box_end`, `end_join_list_not_read`; a curve end that lists walls and lies at neither end of the box is the first too, and an end whose list is empty is not read and carries no marker), or where the cut of the base resolves and its solid cannot be made (`base_solid_not_made_over_the_extended_plan`). `BodyEndSource` is `wall_end_stepped_to_joined_walls` and `EndCoverElementId` the least id of the walls followed where the end moves. The two RE-337 values (`wall_end_to_base_wall_upper_step_omitted`, `wall_end_extended_to_base_wall`), and its reasons `wall_beneath_not_one_rectangle` and `walls_beneath_reach_the_end_at_different_faces`, are gone.
