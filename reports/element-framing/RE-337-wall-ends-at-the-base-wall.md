## 3. The rule

What the walls share that the walls beside them do not is the edited elevation profile (a `WallProfileOpening` voids each) and a record box whose end lies inside the wall beneath, cut back by a join trim. For a wall with an edited profile that is a plain rectangle along a model axis (to 1e-6 of a quarter turn) and has `JoinTrimStart` and `JoinTrimEnd`, an end is drawn to the face of the other rectangles along the perpendicular axis that hold the wall's base (the wall stands on them, not on their top: `base < top - 0.001 ft`), span its width and hold the end of the record box (the body's end moved out by the trim), where they stand beyond the body's end by no more than their own thickness, where no such wall touches the end already, and where they all end at one face (within 0.001 ft). A beneath wall is a plain rectangle, or one whose top a floor cut in steps (RE-320), taken as the whole of its plan to the lowest underside. The pass runs on the bodies after the tops are cut and before the bases are, so that the walls beneath have their final tops; the tops pass leaves a wall with an edited profile alone, so it is not changed by the order.

**The end is a block, stepped under the walls in its way.** Revit's end steps up the height, and which of the walls that meet it at a height it follows is its join order, which is not read. The block added at an end is drawn up to the lowest of: the top of the walls it reaches, the base of any other wall the pass reads whose plan it overlaps by more than 0.001 ft on both axes and that stands above the wall's base (the block must not lie inside a wall; a wall that is turned off a quarter turn or drawn as another kind of solid is not read and does not stop it), and the wall's own top. The steps above are not drawn. That is a bound by the geometry the pass reads, not Revit's rule, and whether a wall is drawn above Revit's at an added end is measured (section 5), not derived. Where such a wall stands in the block from the wall's own base up, the end is kept (`wall_stands_in_the_added_end`). The body is the wall's own plan to its top and one block for each end, as the items of one `Compound` (two `IfcFacetedBrep`s on the sloped footing of RE-336); the wall's `extrusion` stays its full plan to its top, which the passes that read a wall's plan use. A wall whose base is cut gets its body from the base pass, with a top for each piece, and the base pass refuses to make it if any piece is dropped; one that is not gets it from `finish_wall_ends`, whose first item is the whole plan to the lowest top of its pieces, as RE-320's stepped body is, so the glTF lays the type's layers across it, and whose cut openings attach (RE-321, `BodyEndSource` counts as a stepped body there).

**The openings stay put.** The origin of the wall moves to the middle of the longer plan and the openings of its edited profile, which are placed in its frame, are moved back by the shift (their outline along their axis and their plane offset to its left), so each stays where it was in model space.

**The base must still be cut.** A wall whose base join names elements (`m_wall_base_cutters`, RE-336) takes the longer plan only if the decision of RE-336 for its base (`decide_base`) is a cut over it and its solid, with the blocks, can be made: the pass tries the ends on a copy of the wall, asks again, and moves nothing if the answer is not a cut. A wall whose base join names nothing has no base to lose and takes the end. Both ends of a wall are decided together.

A wall that takes an end carries `BodyEndSource` and `EndCoverElementId` (the least ElementId of the walls that reach the faces). `BodyEndSource` is `wall_end_to_base_wall_upper_step_omitted` where a block stops below the wall's own top, which is the case on all four measured walls, and `wall_end_extended_to_base_wall` only where every block reaches the wall's full height (which no measured wall does). **The first is a labelled approximation** (Griffin, 2026-11-05 at about 12:30 AM EST, in answer to a question asked with the question tool: ship it, labelled): Revit's end goes on up to the faces of the walls above and those steps are not drawn, so the end is the face of the wall beneath up to the stop of section 3 and the gap, measured in section 5, is Revit's step above it. `JoinTrimStart` and `JoinTrimEnd` stay the record's: once `BodyEndSource` is set the body's end is the wall beneath's face, not the box's end less the trim. A wall that keeps an end the rule would have drawn carries one `BodyEndRefusal`, counted in `exported.wall_end_refusals` and, under `--export-decisions`, as the decision `wall_end_extension_refused` (stage `body_left_as_box`, `detail` the reason). A property set holds one property of a name, so a wall has one reason, and a wall with one end drawn and the other kept carries `BodyEndSource` and `BodyEndRefusal` both.

- `wall_beneath_not_one_rectangle`: a wall beneath that would reach the end has a profile of its own (a stepped or layered one), whose face is not read, whether or not its top was cut in steps. The BIM4LCA office at building permit: 2058499 and 2058501.
- `base_not_cut_over_the_extended_plan`: the base join names elements and none cuts the base over the longer plan, as before it. The office as built: 2058499 and 2058501.
- `base_cut_lost_on_extended_plan`: the base was cut on the plan the record gives and would not be on the longer plan. **No measured model needed it: this reason has not been exercised against a Revit export.**
- `walls_beneath_reach_the_end_at_different_faces`: the walls that hold the base end at faces more than 0.001 ft apart. **No measured model needed it: this reason has not been exercised against a Revit export.**
- `wall_stands_in_the_added_end`: another wall stands in the stretch the end would be drawn over, from the wall's own base up. **No measured model needed it: this reason has not been exercised against a Revit export.**

A wall with no join trim, a body that is not a record box, a beneath wall that is a solid of another kind (a base or top cut that is not RE-320's stepped body), or a wall that is not profile-edited is left as it was, with no marker: the rule never considered it, and a beneath wall of another kind is an uncounted skip. The markers are in `RvtElementRecordGeometry`, not in a `Pset_` set, so `decoded.parameter_value_count` does not count them (54,952 as built and 55,145 at permit, equal to `main`'s, as measured on 2026-11-04; #1424 has since left out of this count the `Pset_` values of the 47 folded `IfcCovering` pieces on each file, which the writer does not emit, and Measure run 41989882391 reads 54,764 as built and 54,957 at permit, 188 fewer on each file).
