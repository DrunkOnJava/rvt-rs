## 3. The rule

What the walls share that the walls beside them do not is the edited elevation profile (a `WallProfileOpening` voids each) and a record box whose end lies inside the wall beneath, cut back by a join trim. For a wall with an edited profile that is a plain rectangle along a model axis (to 1e-6 of a quarter turn) and has `JoinTrimStart` and `JoinTrimEnd`, an end is drawn to the face of the other plain rectangles along the perpendicular axis that hold the wall's base (the wall stands on them, not on their top: `base < top - 0.001 ft`), span its width and hold the end of the record box (the body's end moved out by the trim), where they stand beyond the body's end by no more than their own thickness, where no such wall touches the end already, and where they all end at one face (within 0.001 ft). It runs on the built bodies, before the tops and bases are cut.

**The end is stepped.** Revit's end steps up the height. The block added at an end is drawn up to the top of the wall it reaches (the lowest of them, where several reach one face) and no higher, where that is below the wall's top; the steps above, at 183.071 ft for 2057668, are not drawn, so the body is under-drawn there and never over-drawn. The body is the wall's own plan to its top and one block for each end, as the items of one `Compound` (two `IfcFacetedBrep`s on the sloped footing of RE-336); the wall's `extrusion` stays its full plan to its top, which the passes that read a wall's plan use. A wall whose base is cut gets it from the base pass, with a top for each piece; one that is not gets it from `finish_wall_ends`.

**The openings stay put.** The origin of the wall moves to the middle of the longer plan and the openings of its edited profile, which are placed in its frame, are moved back by the shift (their outline along their axis and their plane offset to its left), so each stays where it was in model space.

**The base must still be cut.** A wall whose base join names elements (`m_wall_base_cutters`, RE-336) takes the longer plan only if the decision of RE-336 for its base (`decide_base`) is a cut over it and its solid, with the blocks, can be made: the pass tries the ends on a copy of the wall, asks again, and moves nothing if the answer is not a cut. A wall whose base join names nothing has no base to lose and takes the end. Both ends of a wall are decided together.

A wall that takes an end carries `BodyEndSource` `wall_end_extended_to_base_wall` and `EndCoverElementId` (the least ElementId of the walls that reach the faces). `JoinTrimStart` and `JoinTrimEnd` stay the record's: once `BodyEndSource` is set the body's end is the wall beneath's face, not the box's end less the trim. A wall that keeps an end the rule would have drawn carries one `BodyEndRefusal`, counted in `exported.wall_end_refusals` and, under `--export-decisions`, as the decision `wall_end_extension_refused` (stage `body_left_as_box`, `detail` the reason). A property set holds one property of a name, so a wall has one reason, and a wall with one end drawn and the other kept carries `BodyEndSource` and `BodyEndRefusal` both.

- `wall_beneath_not_one_rectangle`: a wall beneath that would reach the end has a profile of its own (a stepped or layered one), whose face is not read. The BIM4LCA office at building permit: 2058499 and 2058501.
- `base_not_cut_over_the_extended_plan`: the base join names elements and none cuts the base over the longer plan, as before it. The office as built: 2058499 and 2058501.
- `base_cut_lost_on_extended_plan`: the base was cut on the plan the record gives and would not be on the longer plan. **No measured model needed it: this reason has not been exercised against a Revit export.**
- `walls_beneath_reach_the_end_at_different_faces`: the walls that hold the base end at faces more than 0.001 ft apart. **No measured model needed it: this reason has not been exercised against a Revit export.**

A wall with no join trim, a body that is not a record box, a beneath wall that is not plain (a base cut, a solid), or a wall that is not profile-edited is left as it was, with no marker: the rule never considered it. The markers are in `RvtElementRecordGeometry`, not in a `Pset_` set, so `decoded.parameter_value_count` does not count them (54,764 as built and 54,971 at permit, equal to `main`'s).
