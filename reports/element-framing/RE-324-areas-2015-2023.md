# RE-324: Areas on Revit 2015 to 2023

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Issues:** #1194 (Areas beyond Revit 2024), #1261 (BIM Whale's Areas); follows RE-191 (the Area records), RE-315 (the 32-bit records) and RE-317 (an Area's boundary)
**Artefacts:** Autodesk's `rac_advanced_sample_project` of 2015 to 2023 and `rst_basic_sample_project` of 2016 to 2023, each with the same project saved by Revit 2024 as the oracle (`research/autodesk-sample-projects.tsv`, fetched by Measure's `-f samples=true`, never stored; hashes below); the held-out BIM Whale `AdvancedProject` (Revit 2021), by counts only
**Probe:** `probes/examples/probe_re324_area_records_32bit.rs`
**Runs:** the probe, [41754347940](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/41754347940) (b2c9e26b, the records before any reader change); the reader that writes every placed Area, [41756655307](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/41756655307) (5968ffde) and [41757736776](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/41757736776) (c53dc684, adding the outlines); that reader unfiltered, [41757734285](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/41757734285) (c53dc684 against `main` at 44b0b46c), BIM Whale's first measurement; the narrowed rule on the samples, [41763339272](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/41763339272) (c77faa68); the narrowed rule unfiltered, [41764492879](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/41764492879) (9e05f179 against `main` at 8b8f1fee)
**Status:** positive on Autodesk's samples, by agreement with the same project's 2024 save. **The first rule, writing every placed Area as 2024 does, is a measured negative on the held-out BIM Whale project: it found Revit's 8 spaces and wrote 2 that Revit's export lacks** (§6, counts only; the cause is not measured). The rule is narrowed: a 2015 to 2023 Area is written only where its region and body are proven (§7), and BIM Whale is a regression check under W5, quoted by counts and never developed on. No Revit export of any 2015 to 2023 file with Areas is scored.

## 1. Before

Areas were read on Revit 2024 only (RE-191), with bodies from their boundary lines (RE-317). On 2015 to 2023 `partition_area_records` returned nothing, and the 32-bit path (`recover_2023_records`) never asked for Areas: no `IfcSpace` was written for one. Autodesk's `rac_advanced` holds 25 Areas in every save from 2015 to 2024 and `rst_basic` one from 2016 to 2024; BIM Whale's 2021 project exports 8 `IfcSpace` that are Areas, none of which rvt-rs wrote (RE-317 §5).

## 2. The 32-bit Area record

The partition walk of RE-315 (`record_spans`) returns an Area's chain record, a declared record whose `BuiltInCategory` is `OST_Areas` (-2003200). The probe finds the category by the offset at which -2003200 occurs in the first 40 bytes of each record: 14, on all 25 Areas of every `rac_advanced` save and the one of every `rst_basic` save (the probe's `category slot` line prints the histogram, `{14: 25}`). Every Area record, on every save 2015 to 2023, is 119 bytes and holds:

| Offset | Content | Value on every Area |
|---|---|---|
| +0 | `u32` ElementId, `u32` size (119), the tag of `ElementHeader`, a `u16` count | |
| +14 | `i64` `BuiltInCategory` | -2003200 |
| +26 | `u32` design option (RE-315: 12 bytes after the category) | `ff ff ff ff`, the main model |
| +30 | `u32` container reference (16 bytes after the category) | `ff ff ff ff`, unset |
| +38 | `u32` placement kind (24 bytes after the category) | `0xffffef7f`, the placed-instance kind, **on 2015 to 2018 too**: a Level's is `0xffffef6f` there (RE-181) |
| +0x3a | `u32` count 6 and six ascending `u32` ElementIds: the Level, the area scheme, 9492, the two boundary lines the Area names, its own id | |
| +0x6a | `u32` count 2 and two ids: the Level and 85092 | |

Where it sits is RE-315's marker-relative constants turned into record-relative ones (id at -52, category -38, design option -26, container -22, placement -14 from the marker). Number and name are RE-117's entries, read by `scan_room_parameter_entries` unchanged. The Level, the scheme and the lines are the ids the counted lists name that are Levels, area schemes and declared area boundary lines; the lists are found as ascending runs of at least two declared ids after the placement word, which gives exactly these two on all 26 Areas.

## 3. The point and the boundary lines

- **The point.** The 2024 point of an Area (two `f64`s, RE-317) is found, as the same 16 bytes, in the older save's partition 35 bytes after the 8-byte header `01 00 00 00 · ElementId` of the Area's element data (the room reader's header). The `u32` that follows that header is the Area's Level. Exact bytes at that offset: 25 of 25 on the 2021, 2022 and 2023 `rac_advanced` saves, 24 of 25 on 2015 to 2020 (Area 12, below), and 1 of 1 on every `rst_basic` save. The offset is the same for every Area on every Level: the probe's header distances are 35 for every Area, plus 5, 7, 2, 6, 2 and 1 further hits at 506 to 645 bytes, which are other copies of the same 16 bytes later in the same element data.
- **The boundary lines.** Each of the 127 area boundary lines of `rac_advanced` (17 of `rst_basic`) is a 32-bit element record that `scan_records` returns; its category, Level and scheme equal the 2024 save's on all 127 (17), and its box on 127 of 127 on the 2022 and 2023 saves and on 17 of 17 of every `rst_basic` save. On the 2015 to 2021 `rac_advanced` saves 111 of 127 boxes equal the 2024 save's (§4).
- **Levels.** Name, elevation and Building Story setting equal the 2024 save's on every Level of every pair (5 on `rac_advanced`, 9 on `rst_basic`, 2015 included), so the Level above is proven as it is on 2024.

## 5. The reader against the 2024 save

This section is the reader that writes every placed Area (§6 measures it on a held-out project; §7 narrows it). `areas_from_partition_chain_records` reads Revit 2015 to 2023 on the admission of 2024: the container unset, the placed-instance kind, the primary design option, the latest partition's copy; the lines from `scan_records`; the point as in §3; every other RE-317 rule unchanged (all declared lines read, point in one region, axis-aligned lines, building-story Levels, redundant Areas refused). **Revit 2016 to 2023 leave out an Area in a non-primary option, as 2024 does; Revit 2015 reads no option sets, so an Area (or a line) that names any option is left out there.** No sample Area names an option. Revit 2008 to 2014 are not read. The probe compares, per Area id, every field the reader returns but its source stream and offset with the 2024 save's reader output (runs 41756655307, 41757736776).

The Areas per save, `rac_advanced` (25 Areas, 127 lines; the 2024 save: 3 bodies, 18 `boundary_lines_not_readable`, 4 `point_in_no_single_region`):

| 2021 | 25 of 25 | 0 (§4: two copies of the 16 lines) | 15 of 25 | 10: 3 bodies refused, 7 refusal reasons |
| 2022, 2023 | 25 of 25 | 25 of 25 | 25 of 25, the 3 bodies included | none |

`rst_basic` (one Area, 17 lines; the 2024 save refuses it `next_storey_not_proven`): 2016 to 2023, 8 saves, everything equal, the reader's output identical, 8 of 8.

- **Where the inputs equal the 2024 save's the output equals it exactly**: all 25 Areas of the 2022 and 2023 saves, 3 bodies among them (every field, profile ring and height), and the Area of all 8 `rst_basic` saves.
- **2015 to 2020, where the inputs differ (counted, not scored).** 24 of 25 outputs are identical anyway, including Areas 23 and 24's bodies and the 18 and 4 refusals, Area 12's among them: it has a point (66.574391, 91.024065) and is refused `boundary_lines_not_readable` as in the 2024 save. Fail closed; the latest copy of the 16 equals the 2024 save's, but taking it would be a new rule for the 2024 save too.

The exporter's source gives one candidate, not measured: revit-ifc's `SpatialElementExporter.CreateIFCSpace` (`IFC_v24.3.40`, CLEANROOM.md source 5) returns no space for an Area with no location, and for one whose boundary yields no curve loop, which is what a redundant or a not-enclosed Area is. rvt-rs writes every placed Area and reads neither whether it encloses an area nor why one has none. ## 7. The narrowed rule

**A Revit 2015 to 2023 Area is written as an `IfcSpace` only where its region and body are proven**, the condition RE-317 uses on 2024 for a body: every declared boundary line read, the point in one region the named lines bound, axis-aligned lines, building-story Levels, no second Area with the region. A proven region is a boundary, which is what Revit's exporter needs to make a space (§6); an Area without one is not written. It is counted instead: `skipped` carries `area_without_proven_body` with the number and, in `classes`, the `AreaBodyRefusal` reason of each (`boundary_lines_not_readable`, `point_in_no_single_region`, `named_lines_not_in_scheme_and_level`, `next_storey_not_proven`, and the others of RE-317). It is not said to be left out by Revit's export. Revit 2024 is unchanged: it writes every placed Area and counts those without a body in `exported.area_body_refusals`.

Measure [41763339272](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/41763339272) (c77faa68, `-f base=main -f samples=true -f sample_years="2015 2023"`, 33 samples): 25 IFC files are byte-identical to `main`'s and 8 differ, `rac_advanced` of 2015 to 2020, 2022 and 2023. On those 8 `IfcSpace` goes from 91 to 94, the 3 bodies of §5, and `skipped` carries `area_without_proven_body` 22 (18 `boundary_lines_not_readable`, 4 `point_in_no_single_region`). The `rac_advanced` 2021 save writes nothing new (`IfcSpace` 91, `skipped` 25: 22 `boundary_lines_not_readable`, 3 `named_lines_not_in_scheme_and_level`) and each of the 8 `rst_basic` saves too (`skipped` 1, `next_storey_not_proven`): their IFC is byte-identical to `main`'s (their `diagnostics.json` gains the `skipped` item: 17 of the 33 samples).

**What the narrowing leaves out.** 22 of `rac_advanced`'s 25 Areas on each of 8 saves (18 whose group holds a line that is not along an axis, 4 bounded partly by walls), all 25 on its 2021 save, and the one `rst_basic` Area on each of 8 saves, which Revit's export probably writes (not measured). **BIM Whale's 8 are left out too, and that is known, not conditional**: run 41757734285's `space profiles` line gives rvt-rs 10 spaces and none with a body, so none of the 10 is a proven Area, and the narrowed rule writes 0 of Revit's 8, as `main` does. The 0 extra spaces of its run (§8) is therefore **vacuous**: the rule writes nothing there, so it does not show that it separates Revit's 8 from the 2 extras. **#1261's BIM Whale Areas stay undelivered**, and cannot be developed further on that file: under W5 it is a regression check, quoted by counts, never developed on. No Revit export of any 2015 to 2023 file with Areas exists in the corpus, so the 3 bodies the rule writes (`rac_advanced`, 8 saves) are checked only against the 2024 save's reader output, which RE-317 measured against Revit's export on the BIM4LCA office, not on this model.

## 9. Not measured

- Any Revit export of a 2015 to 2023 file with Areas: the BIM Whale counts of §6 and §8 are counts, and the sample projects have no Revit export. The 3 bodies written are checked against the 2024 save's reader output only.
- BIM Whale's 8 Areas (#1261): not written, so nothing about them is measured under the narrowed rule; the zero extras of §8 is vacuous.
- `Pset_SpaceCommon.IsExternal` and the other named parameters of a 2015 to 2023 Area space: 2024 attaches them (`attach_native_named_parameters`, `attach_record_is_external`) and the 32-bit path does not yet; a run of the samples with them is not made, so they are not written.
- Why 2 of BIM Whale's 10 written spaces are not in Revit's export (§6): which Areas, and whether Revit's exporter drops them for having no enclosed boundary, for their "Export to IFC As" value or for the view the export took. Revit's export of an Area depends on conditions rvt-rs does not read (location, boundary loops, the export filter), and every sample and office Area that has a Revit export is exported.
- Releases other than the ones above: 2008 to 2014 are not read; 2015 reads no design option sets.
- A design option on any Area: none of the 26 sample Areas names one, so the non-primary rule on 2016 to 2023 is Revit 2024's, not measured on a 32-bit Area. An option of a set that could not be resolved counts as primary there, as on 2024.
- Revit's phase filter, which skips a spatial element in an invalid phase (RE-184): not applied to an Area on any release, as on 2024.
- Everything RE-317 lists as not measured: the top storey, Levels that are not building stories, a Level's Story Above, an Area's base offset, boundary lines that are not along the axes, Areas bounded partly by walls (4 of `rac_advanced`'s 25), and `Qto_SpaceBaseQuantities.Height`.
- The point of an Area whose copies in one partition disagree is not read.
