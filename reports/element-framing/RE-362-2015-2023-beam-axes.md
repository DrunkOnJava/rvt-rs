# RE-362: Revit 2015 to 2023 beams along their location lines

**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Issues:** #1190 (geometry beyond the box on 2015 to 2023, 2026 and 2027); follows RE-49 (the beam's bounded line), RE-114, RE-180 and RE-181 (the same line record on a wall, 2015 to 2023), RE-276 (a beam's rotation), RE-280 (native records on 2015 to 2022), RE-341 (the same reader on 2026 and 2027).
**Artefacts:** Autodesk's own sample projects, pinned in `research/autodesk-sample-projects.tsv` and fetched by `tools/fetch-autodesk-samples.sh`, never stored: `rac_advanced` 2015 to 2024, `rst_basic` 2015 to 2024, `rst_advanced` 2015 and 2019.
**Scorer:** `tools/re/save_pair_bodies.py` (box and volume, not shape equality), on `main` since RE-341.
**Status:** positive against the same samples saved by a later release. **No Revit export scores any beam this rule draws on 2015 to 2023, among the models Measure fetches without a held-out pair**, and a later save cannot catch an error it shares. Measure run 42134881703 (head 0c39def2, `base=main`, `bimcollab=true`, `racadv=true`, `samples=true` for 2015 to 2024, counts only) scored the two sources of a Revit export that hold such beams: BIMcollab's Revit 2015 example holds 91 beams in Revit's export, and rvt-rs draws all 91 as record boxes, on `main` and on this head (0 drawn by line; `Span` and `Slope` missing on all 91, as on `main`, where Revit writes both); the three.js IFC2X3 export of `rac_advanced`, which the `racadv-2015` to `racadv-2020` models are scored against, holds no `IfcBeam` (it writes 3,308 `IfcMember`, 1,349 `IfcPlate`, 176 `IfcColumn` and 75 `IfcBuildingElementProxy`), and none of the 38 beams has an element with its Tag in it under any class. The held-out 2018 to 2023 models are not in that run.

## 1. Before

`recover_partition_schema_mvp` takes 2015 to 2023 down a branch of its own that ends before the beam readers, so a beam there was its record box, with no `Span`, `Slope`, `Roll` or `Length`, even though 2015 to 2023 walls read the same line record. On `main`, of `exported.body_sources`:

| | `rac_advanced` 2023 / 2024 | `rst_basic` 2023 / 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| `partition_beam_axis` | 0 / 29 | 0 / 345 |
| `partition_beam_box_along_axis` | 0 / 8 | 0 / 9 |

## 2. The change

The 2015 to 2023 branch calls `attach_beam_rotations` and `attach_beam_axes`, nothing else. The line is read as a wall's is. The rotation's native read names `NATIVE_RELEASES`, since its default stops at 2023 (RE-280 measured the framing on 2015 to 2022). `BEAM_AXIS_SUPPORTED_REVIT_VERSIONS` (2024 to 2027) is left alone: it also gates `scan_bounded_lines` and the probes that list a line's copies, which aren't measured on these releases (since #1452 the sketch curves and shafts have lists of their own), so 2015 to 2023 get a list of their own for beams only. On 2024 to 2027 `attach_beam_axes` reads what it read: `scan_bounded_lines` is that gate and then `scan_first_bounded_lines`, which it now calls after its own gate, and the native read of the rotation names the releases 2015 to 2027 where it named 2023 to 2027. 2008 to 2014 stay as they were.

Why Revit's upgrade moved them is not decoded.

## 4. Within each file

Every beam's new body is within 3 mm of its own save's record box, the worst 0.001 m, on all 20 `rac_advanced`, `rst_basic` and `rst_advanced` files of 2015 to 2023. That covers each difference in section 3. The reader requires it (a line's ends must lie in the box), so it shows only that the differences belong to the saves, not that the box is right.

## 5. What else changes

- **Column cuts stay.** RE-122's trim, measured on one 2023 project against Revit's export, keeps precedence: its line leaves the cut box, so none is attached. That leaves 31 beams on `rst_basic` and 4 on `rac_advanced` as they were on `main`, without `Span`, `Roll` or `Length`, where 2024 draws them along their lines.
- **Properties.** `Pset_BeamCommon.Span`, `Slope`, `Roll` and `Qto_BeamBaseQuantities.Length` are written where the line is, at 1e-3 of the later save's on every value except `rst_basic` 2016's `Span` and `Length` on the 13 beams above. Per file, `rac_advanced` 25, 25, 25 and 25; `rst_basic` 320, 306, 224 and 320 (307, 306, 224 and 307 on 2016 equal); `rst_advanced` 54, 42, 42 and 54.
- **`Roll`** is written only for a rotation of exactly 0, as elsewhere; RE-276 measured the value at 0 only.
- **Storeys and GlobalIds** of every beam are as on `main`.
- **Byte identity.** Measure run 42134881703 compared the head's exports with `main`'s by hash, for Autodesk's samples of 2015 to 2024 and the `racadv` and BIMcollab models: 26 move, and nothing else. They are 20 sample saves (`rac_advanced` 2015 to 2023, `rst_basic` 2015 to 2023, `rst_advanced` 2015 and 2019) and the 6 `racadv` models (`racadv-2015` to `racadv-2020`, the same files as the 2015 to 2020 `rac_advanced` saves). No 2024 export moves (the five Autodesk projects and Snowdon's five), nor `rac_basic`, `rme_advanced`, `rme_basic` or BIMcollab. 2025 to 2027 were not in that run. The contributor's local count was 11 of 206 files, "2017 to 2023", which section 3's own beams on 2015 and 2016 contradict.
- **Against Revit's exports** (Measure run 42134881703): none of the beams this rule draws is in a Revit export. On the `racadv` models, `Span` and `Slope` are written on 25 beams that the three.js export does not carry (0 on beams it does carry), and `Roll` on 25, an IFC4-only property that an IFC2X3 export cannot hold: that is what the rule writes there, with nothing to score it against. On BIMcollab it writes nothing, since it draws none of its 91 beams. `box_bodies_vs_ifc` counts the `racadv` beams that stay record boxes at 34 on `main` and 1 on this head, each with no element of that Tag in Revit's export.
- **Cost.** Under 0.15 s on a 1.2 s export (`rst_basic` 2023, 2019): the beams' native read.

- **Why BIMcollab's 91 beams read no line** (Measure run 42139236978, head 207e4130, `-f bimcollab=true -f models="bimcollab-*" -f probe=probe_re362_beam_lines_pre2023`, counts only). The probe is `probe_re328_beam_rejections` on the releases this change reads. On the Revit 2015 file: the release gate passes (`beam_axis_read_on`), every stream is read, and there are 91 structural-framing elements, each with a record box (`no_bbox` 0) and element data found in a partition (`no_data` 0). **All 91 are `no_line`: no copy of any beam's element data holds a bounded line record** in the window the reader takes (from the element-data header to the next one, at most 0x10000 bytes). So the first failing test is the absence of the line, 91 of 91; no beam has copies that disagree (`disagree` 0) or one line (`one_line` 0), so the box tests (`beam_check`, `beam_body`) never run and refuse nothing.
- **What that says about the reach.** The line record the reader takes (RE-49, RE-114: the bytes `04 00 08 01` and a bounded line) is what Autodesk's 2015 samples hold for most of their beams that RE-122 does not cut: 33 of 34 on `rac_advanced`, 29 of 39 on `rst_basic`, 54 of 64 on `rst_advanced` (the rest are record boxes with no line read, as are 333 of `rst_advanced`'s 397 and 120 of `rst_basic`'s 159, which RE-122 cuts). BIMcollab's structural model, the one 2015 structural file from outside Autodesk's samples that Measure fetches, holds none in 91. So the reader's reach on 2015 is Autodesk's samples, and it is not shown to read a 2015 structural model from anywhere else: either that file stores a beam's location line in another record or form that is not decoded, or the reader's window or tag does not reach it. The probe says the line is absent from the window, not where it is. The same holds for 2016 to 2023: the reach is measured on the same samples only, and no model from outside them has been read (the held-out 2018 to 2023 models are in the unfiltered run). No fix is made: there is no one-line change to measure, since nothing shows where BIMcollab's line is. A candidate not tried is the native `FamInstCurveDriver` record, which the rotation already reads on 2015 (RE-280), and the curve a wall takes from its native record (#1475), for the beam's location curve.

## 6. Not measured, not read

- Against a Revit export: see the status above. Where BIMcollab's line is: not known (section 5).
- A beam's I section (RE-103) was not tried. `type_i_sections_at` is limited to 2024 and later, and it looks for entries of `f64`, eight `ff` and an `i64` parameter; whether a 32-bit file stores them that way was not looked at.
- `tools/re/bodies_across_saves.py` and Measure's gate do not hold these saves; they cover 2026 and 2027.
- Sketch lines, arcs, circles and shaft lines on 2015 to 2023: another slice.

