## 1. What was sent

STE1200 (Steffen, SLIK Architekten) posted on 2026-10-29 that four small changes let the saved-graphics reader read a Revit 2020 family whose bodies are imported geometry only: no sketch, no DirectShape, 36 `ImportInstance` elements on 29 `MasterImportSymbol` elements with no CAD source, so that the geometry exists only in the saved graphics. On 2026-11-02 the maintainer asked whether the patch was offered under Apache 2.0 and whether the family's terms allowed an automated fetch. On 2026-11-03 STE1200 answered that the patch and the example are offered under Apache 2.0, and that the family's terms (BIMobject's, as the contributor reads them: no passing on to third parties, no access outside the Bim.com interface) do not allow it; the maintainer removed the link and said the family would not enter the repository or CI. At 14:57 UTC on 2026-11-03 STE1200 attached a zip with only the patch, moved to `a98bd316`, and a per-instance JSON, both under Apache 2.0.

The zip (sha256 `1fdbb1f0…c6e1` as downloaded on 2026-11-05) holds `rvt-rs_a98bd316_revit2020_saved_graphics.patch` (15,958 bytes) and `vectors_revit2020_import_instances.json` (19,836 bytes). It was read as data and nothing in it was run. The patch applies to `main` at `ce0b2398` with offsets of 72 and 263 lines and no conflict (`git apply --check`).

## 2. The family, and what is not in this repository

The family is a manufacturer's lift family that a configurator generates, saved by Revit 2020. It is not downloaded, read, stored or named here beyond the contributor's public figures; the thread has its name, hash and source. What this report and its PR carry of it are counts the contributor published in the thread: 2,204 element records in one partition, 17 groups, 36 `ImportInstance` elements on 29 `MasterImportSymbol` elements, 325 `GPolyMesh` objects with 47,968 facets and, placed, 48,520 triangles, 109 deferred history episodes, and 36 `GInstance` objects with `m_bHasScale` set. The per-instance JSON is not committed: it names the family's symbols and holds its geometry, and nothing CI can fetch reads it.

## 3. Provenance and licence

The contributor wrote on 2026-08-21 that the work is "clean-room black-box work on publicly available files — no decompilation, no NDA material", and on 2026-08-30 that language models, given matching IFC files, did the decoding. The patch reads as that says: every class and field it names (`m_oEpisodes`, `Episode`, `FacetedTopology9`, `m_normalsFlag`, `m_edgeVisFlagsArr`, `InstanceInfo`, `m_GRepId`, `m_bHasScale`) is a name in the file's own `Formats/Latest` schema (CLEANROOM.md, accepted source 1), its comments cite only measurements on the one family, and the independent reader the contributor checked against was written from this repository's `native_segments.rs`. Nothing in it points to a forbidden source. This report did not check the contributor's statements about how the file was obtained or decoded beyond reading the patch for such a reference, and found none.

### 5.2 The sample projects against their Revit 2023 downloads

A `FamilyInstance`, `ImportInstance` or `DirectShape` element is meshed as `saved_meshes::attach` meshes a body (detail level 3, `native_saved_scene`), and counted drawn whole when its status is `decoded_saved_graphics` with at least one primitive. Two elements are equal when the triangle count is the same, the world box differs by at most 0.0001 ft and the total surface area differs by at most 0.0001 of the 2023 file's: the probe's one constant, `TOLERANCE_FEET`, bounds the box difference in feet and, as the same number, the area difference as a dimensionless ratio. "Same triangles" is the same triangle count; equal adds the box and the total area. Neither compares a vertex, so two meshes that agree in all of these can still differ in where their vertices are. ### 5.3 What the three changes left out do to these readings

Run 42013687044 at `ecc302d2` read the same pairs with all four of the patch's changes (the `m_bHasScale` clause narrowed to a rigid matrix, `FacetedTopology0` and `9` with normal binding 2, the deferred episodes). Its drawn-whole, whole-in-both, same-triangle-count, equal and not-equal counts equal run 42015502520's at `ab9503b1`, which has only the `InstanceInfo` change, row for row and file for file (51,248 elements, 45,649 drawn whole, 45,349 in both, 41,687 equal). Its stop histograms do not: 15 of the 24 pair files differ, all in the older file's scene (the 2023 copies are the same). In the 7 `rst_basic` pairs of 2016 to 2022, 3 owners per file move from `scaled instance mode is not qualified` to `unsupported reachable graphics class GText`, and the owners with no primitive fall by 1 (70 to 69). In the 8 `rac_advanced` pairs of 2015 to 2022, one owner per file gains a `GText` stop and the owners with no primitive fall by 1 (326 to 325 on 2015 to 2020, 335 to 334 on 2021 and 2022), while the `scaled instance mode` stop that `main` shows on one owner of each stays. So the narrowed `m_bHasScale` clause (rotation, mirror and identity matrices pass; a uniform scale stays refused) moves owners past the scale check to a later stop, a `GText`, and one owner per file gains a primitive; none becomes drawn whole, since any stop leaves an owner `partial_saved_graphics`. The histogram does not show why the `rac_advanced` owner changes. The 69 flagged instances of 2015 to 2022 are 29 identity, 8 mirror and 32 uniform-scale matrices (section 5.1); the output does not say which flagged instance each moved owner holds. No count of drawn bodies, equal elements or triangles moves in any open file here.

### 5.6 What guards a wrong mesh on Revit 2015 to 2022

`default_representation` is the one change in `src/`, and it has two call sites in `native_graphics_traversal.rs`, lines 371 and 421 (a `GComponentRef`'s `InstanceInfo` and a `GInstance`'s). As first taken (`ab9503b1`) it accepted an `InstanceInfo` with no `m_GRepId` key when `m_Trf` and `m_cda` were present, which checked nothing: `read_transform` of `m_Trf` runs before it at both sites and refuses a missing `m_Trf`, and the `m_cda` test after it refuses a missing `m_cda`. That made a missing key an open guard, where `main` refused anything but 0. `c22ef90d` accepts a missing `m_GRepId` only from an object that holds exactly three keys, `m_Trf`, `m_symbolId` and `m_cda`, which are the fields the classes of all 30 sample projects of 2015 to 2022 declare (`InstInfoBase`: `m_Trf`, `m_symbolId`; `InstanceInfo`: `m_cda`; the probe's `schema` lines in 42015502520). What it adds is only this: an `InstanceInfo` that stores a further field no measured class declares is refused. It is not a test of the class schema, which would be the closed guard: the traversal is handed a decoded graph and no `Registry`, and passing one means a new parameter on every `select_graphics*` and `graphics*` entry point. Nor can it tell a missing key from a field the decoder drops unread (`native_parameters.rs` skips a field whose `uninterpreted_flags & 2` is set), so a class that declared `m_GRepId` with that flag would be read as the default representation. No measured schema does: none of the 30 files of 2015 to 2022 declares the field, and all 62 distinct files of 2023 to 2027 read (59 in 42015507053, the three 2023 samples of 42015502520) declare it, the 44 that hold any `InstanceInfo` storing 0.

The release gate alone keeps a wrong mesh of a 2015 to 2022 file out of every export: `Options::releases` defaults to 2023 to 2027 and `saved_meshes::default_scope` returns none outside it. The reading is a research one in the probe and has no other guard. One exposure lies behind that gate. 271 owners of the 24 older files (35 in each save of 2016 to 2022, 26 in 2015; the 2023 copies have 280) now stop at `VIEW_COMPONENT_DIAGNOSTIC` (`surface-bearing component requires qualified owner/view visibility`), where `main`'s readers stopped 271 owners at `unsupported component representation/document context` (42013683146); the count is the same, and the histograms do not identify the owners, though the branch order (the representation test before the view-component return) makes them likely the same. `saved_meshes::is_partial` tolerates that diagnostic, on the strength of RE-224's 21 exact saved meshes of RE1 Electrical (Revit 2025), so `attach` would draw those owners' meshes without the component's surface if the gate were lifted; the probe's "drawn whole" is a status of `decoded_saved_graphics` (`saved_element_status`: any diagnostic is partial), so owners stopped there are not in the table of section 5.2, and its equal elements say nothing of them. They are unmeasured on 2015 to 2022.

### 9.2 What is taken, and the rule of each

- **Deferred history episodes** (`native_index::creation_episodes`). A root whose `m_episodeList.m_oEpisodes` holds pointers is read when each is an `Episode` reference with the deferred pointer token, their `Episode` bodies follow the root in list order, the counted section that follows repeats their GUIDs in the same order, and four zero bytes close the stream; any other shape, a count over 1,000,000 or GUID lists that differ refuses the history, and `native_document` then refuses the file as it did. The bodies are read for their GUID and nothing else.
- **`FacetedTopology9` under normal binding 2** (`native_saved_mesh`). Only that class under only that binding. `FacetedTopology0` stays read under binding 0 as before; every other class and binding, `FacetedTopology0`, `8` and `1` under binding 2 included, stays refused. The mesh needs one stored normal per facet and `normals_follow_winding`: every stored normal within 0.1 degree (`NORMAL_WINDING_TOLERANCE_DEGREES`) of its facet's winding normal. A facet with collinear corners or an index out of range, a stored normal of no length or not a number, or a count that differs from the facets' refuses the whole mesh (`stored normals do not follow the winding`) and leaves its owner partial. The stored normal is never taken unchecked, and a mesh that fails is not drawn from the winding instead. 0.1 degree is the bound the contributor's 47,968 facets all meet; the probe's second bucket, 1 degree, is not used. `m_edgeVisFlagsArr` is not read.
- **A set `m_bHasScale`** (`native_graphics_traversal`). It is read only on a `GInstance` that names an `InstanceInfo` of exactly `m_Trf`, `m_symbolId` and `m_cda` (no `m_GRepId`, section 5.6) whose 3x3 is a rotation: orthonormal rows within 1e-6 and a positive determinant, the identity included. The first version (`f58d4710`) read any orthonormal matrix, mirrors included; it is narrowed to the 26 identity and 10 rotation matrices the contributor's 36 instances hold. A mirror or any scale stays refused, and so does the flag with an `InstanceInfo` that has `m_GRepId`, which is every file of Revit 2023 to 2027 (the 2024 office's 10 sanitary terminals of RE-329 §1 stay refused for it).

What guards a wrong result of the third. It is read only on the older schema and only with a matrix that moves nothing the matrix does not say. What the flag means there is not read: if it marks a scale that the matrix does not carry, a mesh drawn through it is wrong, and nothing in the file says so. On the open files, 69 flagged instances of 2015 to 2022 are 29 identity, 8 mirror and 32 uniform scale (section 5.1), and the held-out models hold 1,592 flagged instances in 3 pre-2023 models (350 identity, 187 rotation, 552 mirror, 503 uniform scale); the narrowing leaves the mirrors and the scales refused, which is 40 of the 69 and 1,055 of the 1,592. Run 42029562666 found 57 owners of one held-out model that pass the scale check and end `decoded_saved_graphics` with no primitive, and could not say what they hold. They were read with every orthonormal matrix allowed; with rotations only, run 42039914315 says how many still pass (section 9.3).

### 9.4 What nothing in CI will catch

The three changes are reachable on no file CI fetches: 0 deferred pointers in 92 open files, 18 held-out models and 13 families; 0 `FacetedTopology9` objects in the 92 open files and the 18 held-out models; and no body drawn through a set flag (sections 5.1, 5.3 and 5.5). The only file that exercises them is the contributor's, and the check is theirs to repeat.

What does run in CI is the rule of each guard as a pure function, with its failure modes tested (`cargo test --lib`, the exception of CONTRIBUTING.md for a pure helper): `normals_follow_winding` (a normal opposite to the winding, tilted past the tolerance, of no length, not a number, a facet with collinear corners or a corner out of range, fewer normals than facets, one bad facet among good ones), `rotation` (mirrors, a scale, a shear, a value that is not a number) and `default_representation`. They prove the rule, not that a file is read right.

A synthetic fixture for the deferred-episode path is possible and is not built here. It would splice, into the `Global/History` of a Revit 2023 or later file CI fetches (Core Interior, MIT), the deferred pointers and `Episode` bodies the counted section already holds, and assert that `creation_episodes` returns the same GUIDs, and refuses when one differs. It needs the wire form of a deferred pointer in `m_oEpisodes`, which only the contributor's family shows, and as decoded values, not bytes; a fixture built on a guess would prove the guess. The family itself never enters the repository, CI or a report beyond the counts the contributor published.
