- **A door's opening in a second wall is written on Revit 2024 only (#1165,
  RE-353).** Once the door cuts of Revit 2015 to 2023 were read (RE-450), the
  rule wrote one such opening on a Revit 2018 file and one each on two
  held-out files of 2018 and 2021, and Revit's exports hold none of them. The
  rule is measured on Revit 2024's BIM4LCA files only: no Revit export of 2015
  to 2023 holds such an opening, and none of 2025 to 2027 has a door that cuts
  a second wall. On every other release the step is counted as
  `door_second_wall_release_not_measured`.
