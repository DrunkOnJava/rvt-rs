- **A curtain mullion turned off the model's axes is measured along its own
  axes down to a nearer-degenerate turn (#1193).** The 12 short mullions of the
  BIM4LCA office, shorter than their own section and turned some 47 degrees,
  get their `Span` and `Qto_MemberBaseQuantities.Length` from the box along

  degrees of 45 keeps RE-226's rule.
