- **A door or window whose symbol cuts no outline carries its overall size
  (#810, #844).** Such a door or window was written with `$`, or with its
  type's Width and Height, which on the BIM4LCA office are 10 mm short of
  Revit's. Where it is set in a wall or a curtain wall, its `OverallWidth`
  and `OverallHeight` are now its own extents, along its axis and through
  its height. On the office all 269 windows and 258 of 269 doors are now
  Revit's (none and 187 before), and so is RE1 Architecture's curtain-wall
  door.
