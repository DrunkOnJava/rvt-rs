- **Areas of Revit 2015 to 2023 are written as `IfcSpace` where their
  boundary lines prove a body.** The 32-bit partition record (RE-315) of an
  Area is the 2024 one with `u32` ElementIds, and a 2015 to 2023 Area is read
  like a 2024 one: number, name, Level, area scheme, the two boundary lines
  it names, its point, and the region those lines enclose around the point,
  extruded to the Level above (RE-317). On Autodesk's `rac_advanced` of 2015
  to 2023 (25 Areas) and `rst_basic` of 2016 to 2023 (1) every Area has the
  number, name, Level, scheme, lines and point of the same project's 2024
  save, and where the model is the 2024 one (the 2022 and 2023 `rac_advanced`
  and every `rst_basic`) the same body or the same refusal, field for field.
  The 2015 to 2021 `rac_advanced` saves hold an earlier state of the sample
  model (16 north-edge lines 0.0246 ft off, one Area's point as far), and
  the 2021 save holds both states, which makes its groups of lines
  unreadable. An Area is written only where its region and body are
  proven: writing every placed Area, as 2024 does, wrote 2 `IfcSpace` that
  Revit's export lacks on the held-out Revit 2021 BIM Whale project (counts
  only; Revit's 8 found; the cause is not measured). An Area without a
  proven body is not written and is counted by reason in the export
  diagnostics (`skipped`, `area_without_proven_body`): 22 of `rac_advanced`'s
  25 on each of 8 saves (18 in groups with a line that is not along an axis, 4
  bounded partly by walls), all 25 on its 2021 save, and `rst_basic`'s one
  (`next_storey_not_proven`). Not measured: any Revit export of a 2015 to 2023
  file with Areas, a design option on a 32-bit Area, Revit 2008 to 2014
  (not read), and on 2015 an Area that names a design option (left out, since
  2015 reads no option sets) ([docs/rooms.md](docs/rooms.md), #1194, #1261,
  RE-324).
