- **Areas of Revit 2025 to 2027 are written as `IfcSpace`, as on 2024.**
  Their partition chain records are the 2024 ones, but a file saved by Revit
  2025 to 2027 may put a block of 22-byte entries before a record's body,
  counted by the `u16` after its class tag, and the reader now skips it. On Autodesk's
  `rac_advanced` the 2025, 2026 and 2027 saves write the 25 Areas of the
  2024 save, each with the same GlobalId, number, name, storey and body. On
  Snowdon Towers Architectural (2025, measured against a Revit 2025 export of
  it, local only; neither file has a recorded source, sha256 or licence yet,
  so these figures are unconfirmed) all 101 of Revit's Area spaces are
  written, with Revit's GlobalIds, numbers and names and none extra, and 95
  of their 96 bodies have Revit's area within 0.1%. The other body is one
  Revit's export leaves without a shape, and 5 Areas Revit draws have none
  here (#1194, RE-333). On the recorded Autodesk 2024 Snowdon Towers samples
  rvt-rs writes 101 Area spaces (96 with a body) on Architectural and 7 (2
  with a body) on Structural, where main writes none; no Revit export of
  those two is available. The diagnostics gain a warning that counts the
  declared chain records whose entry block does not read (it appears on the
  five Snowdon Towers samples, 2,932 to 14,881 records, and on no other).
