- **Areas of Revit 2025 to 2027 are written as `IfcSpace`, as on 2024.**
  Their partition chain records are the 2024 ones, but a file saved by Revit
  2025 to 2027 may put a block of 22-byte entries before a record's body,
  counted by the `u16` after its class tag, and the reader now skips it. On Autodesk's
  `rac_advanced` the 2025, 2026 and 2027 saves write the 25 Areas of the
  2024 save, each with the same GlobalId, number, name, storey and body. On
  Snowdon Towers Architectural (2025, measured against a Revit 2025 export of
  it, local only) all 101 of Revit's Area spaces are written, with Revit's
  GlobalIds, numbers and names and none extra, and 95 of their 96 bodies have
  Revit's area within 0.1%. The other body is one Revit's export leaves
  without a shape, and 5 Areas Revit draws have none here (#1194, RE-330).
